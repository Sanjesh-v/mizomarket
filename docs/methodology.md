# MizoMarket - Research Methodology

## Research Objective

To understand agricultural market-access challenges in selected
Mizoram clusters and validate the requirements of a market
intelligence platform.

## Phase 1: Secondary Research

Review:
- Government agricultural statistics
- Mizoram economic reports
- Previous New India Internship reports
- Research papers on agricultural value chains
- Existing government schemes and initiatives

Record the source, publication date and relevant findings.

## Phase 2: Primary Research

Proposed exploratory research targets:

- 20-30 farmers across 2-3 clusters
- 5-10 traders, processors or buyers
- Consultations with relevant officials and producer groups

These are proposed targets, not completed interviews or
statistically representative sample sizes.

## Phase 3: Stakeholder Questions

### Farmers
- Which crops do you cultivate?
- How much produce do you sell?
- Who purchases your produce?
- How do you determine selling prices?
- What are the main transport and storage challenges?

### Traders and Buyers
- Which crops do you procure?
- What quantity and quality do you require?
- How do you identify suppliers?
- What affects procurement costs?

### Processors
- What is your processing capacity?
- Is production seasonal?
- What quality standards do you follow?
- What infrastructure constraints exist?

### Officials and Producer Groups
- What support programmes are available?
- How are collection and aggregation organised?
- What gaps remain in implementation?

## Phase 4: Analysis

- Map agricultural value chains.
- Compare recorded prices with source and date.
- Identify logistical and information gaps.
- Assess willingness to use digital tools.
- Prioritise features based on stakeholder needs.

## Phase 5: Prototype Validation

Present the prototype to selected stakeholders where feasible.
Collect feedback and revise the proposed features.

## Research Ethics

- Obtain informed consent before interviews.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
- Anonymise responses before publication.
- Do not publish private contact information.
- Clearly distinguish observations from assumptions.
