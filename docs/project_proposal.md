# MizoMarket - Project Proposal

## Project Title

MizoMarket: Strengthening Agricultural Market Access and
Rural Producer Realisations in Mizoram.

## Overview

MizoMarket is a proposed agricultural market intelligence and
producer-buyer linkage initiative.

It aims to investigate market-access challenges in selected
agricultural clusters and explore a digital platform that
organises useful information for producers, buyers and researchers.

## Proposed Modules

### 1. Producer Directory

A structured information system containing:
- Village
- Crop
- Seasonal availability
- Available quantity
- Producer group information

### 2. Price Intelligence

A system for recording:
- Crop
- Market
- Price per unit
- Observation date
- Information source

All prices must be dated and sourced. The prototype does not
currently provide live market quotations.

### 3. Buyer Discovery

A directory framework for:
- Wholesalers
- Processors
- Retailers
- Institutional buyers

Buyer details must be verified and published only with appropriate
permission.

### 4. Supply Analytics

Visualisations to understand:
- Crop distribution
- Village-level supply
- Seasonal availability
- Recorded market observations

### 5. Market Insights

A research-oriented module to identify questions related to
aggregation, transport, processing and buyer requirements.

## Technology

- Python
- Streamlit
- Pandas
- Plotly
- CSV-based data storage

## Expected Deliverables

1. Value-chain maps
2. Stakeholder research findings
3. Producer and buyer information framework
4. Working demonstration prototype
5. Implementation roadmap

## Important Limitation

The platform is a proposed solution. Its features and usefulness
must be refined through primary research and stakeholder feedback.
