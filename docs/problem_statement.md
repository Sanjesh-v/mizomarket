# MizoMarket - Problem Statement

## Background

Agriculture is an important part of rural livelihoods in Mizoram.
Crops such as ginger, turmeric and oranges provide opportunities
for agricultural production, processing and rural enterprise.

However, the journey from producer to buyer can involve several
stages, including aggregation, processing, quality assessment,
transportation and distribution.

## Problem Being Investigated

MizoMarket proposes to investigate whether and how the following
factors affect agricultural market access:

1. Limited visibility of potential buyers.
2. Fragmented agricultural supply.
3. Seasonal availability and processing constraints.
4. Transportation and aggregation challenges.
5. Differences in prices across markets.
6. Limited access to reliable market information.

These are research hypotheses and should not be treated as
universally established problems.

## Project Objective

To understand agricultural value chains in selected Mizoram
clusters and explore a locally relevant market intelligence
solution that can improve producer-buyer visibility.

## Initial Research Clusters

- East Lungdar: Ginger
- Reiek: Turmeric
- Thenzawl: Orange

These clusters are selected for exploratory research based on
prior internship reports. Their current conditions must be
validated through stakeholder consultations.

## Key Research Questions

- How do farmers currently sell their produce?
- Who are the major buyers and intermediaries?
- How are selling prices determined?
- What are the major transportation constraints?
- Are aggregation and processing facilities accessible?
- Would farmers and buyers use a digital information platform?

## Intended Outcome

A field-informed prototype and implementation roadmap.
