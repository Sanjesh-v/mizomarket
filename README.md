# MizoMarket

### Agricultural Market Intelligence Platform for Mizoram

MizoMarket is a proposed rural agricultural market intelligence
initiative designed to investigate market-access challenges and
explore ways to strengthen producer-buyer linkages in Mizoram.

The project is being developed as part of the New India Internship
initiative.

## Problem Statement

Mizoram has agricultural potential in crops such as ginger,
turmeric and oranges. However, geographical constraints,
transportation challenges, seasonal processing, fragmented
supply chains and limited buyer visibility may affect market access.

MizoMarket aims to investigate these challenges through primary
and secondary research and develop a practical digital prototype.

## Proposed Solution

The platform explores five capabilities:

1. Producer and crop directory
2. Agricultural price intelligence
3. Buyer discovery
4. Supply and market analytics
5. Logistics and aggregation insights

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Streamlit
- Pandas
- Plotly
- NumPy
- CSV datasets

## Research Approach

The proposed research will involve:

- Farmers and producer groups
- Agricultural traders and wholesalers
- Food processors
- Agriculture and horticulture officials

Initial research clusters include East Lungdar, Reiek and Thenzawl.
These locations are proposed research areas, not a representative
sample of Mizoram.

## Repository Structure

- docs: Project documentation
- data: Demonstration datasets
- research: Secondary and primary research
- prototype: Streamlit application
- assets: Images and diagrams

## Project Status

Concept development and demonstration MVP.

The included datasets are synthetic demonstration records.
They do not represent actual farmer listings, verified prices,
buyer commitments or completed field-survey findings.

## Expected Deliverables

- Agricultural value-chain maps
- Producer and buyer information framework
- Market-access bottleneck analysis
- Functional digital prototype
- Implementation roadmap

## Objective

The objective is not to introduce another marketplace.
It is to understand where value is lost in Mizoram's agricultural
supply chains and develop a practical, locally validated way
to improve producer access to markets.
